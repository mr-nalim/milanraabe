In short

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking services and embeds no content from other providers. Fonts and images are served from the same server as the site itself.

Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is served via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When the site is accessed, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular the IP address of the device, date and time, the requested address and information about browser and operating system. This serves to deliver the site and keep it secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of the site.

Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. According to GitHub, such transfers are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact by email

If you get in touch by email, the details provided (email address, name, content of the message) are stored to handle the enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where an exhibition or purchase is concerned, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The data are deleted once they are no longer needed and no statutory retention periods apply.

The mailbox is hosted by Gmail (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Instagram

The reference to Instagram is a plain link. Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited) is only contacted when the link is clicked; its own privacy policy then applies.

Your rights

Data subjects have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). An email to the address above is sufficient. There is also a right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner.
